# Sagility Phase 2 — Pharma Commercialization Adjacency

## Overview

This is a strategic playbook for **Sagility** to enter the **Pharma Commercialization** market as a new customer adjacency. It covers prioritization, customer targeting, value proposition, vendor landscape, GTM strategy, talent, and technology investments.

---

## 1. Strategic Thesis

Sagility should enter Pharma Commercialization by leveraging its existing healthcare operations capabilities (contact centers, compliance workflows, AI/automation) and position as a **cost-efficient, execution-focused alternative** to strategy-heavy incumbents like IQVIA, Indegene, ZS Associates, and Syneos Health.

---

## 2. Priority Service Lines (Wedge Strategy)

| Priority | Service Line | Rationale |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Primary** | **Commercial Sales & Marketing** | Strongest capability alignment — high-volume, SLA-driven ops (content production, MLR coordination, CRM admin, analytics). High customer openness to new vendors. |
| **Primary** | **Patient Engagement** | Growing criticality with specialty/rare disease therapies. Rule-based, HIPAA-compliant workflows suit Sagility's contact center DNA. Moderate-to-high openness for new vendors. |
| **Secondary** | **Medical Affairs** | Selective entry potential — requires PharmD/PhD credibility. Content production & MI governance as initial footholds. |
| **Deprioritize** | **Pharmacovigilance** | Conservative market, high regulatory sensitivity, entrenched vendors, limited appetite for new entrants. |

---

## 3. Target Customer Segments

- **Biotech** and **Small-to-Mid Pharma** are the most actionable segments — they have leaner infrastructure, greater openness to modular partners, and lower reference thresholds.
- **Large Pharma** prefers established best-of-breed vendors; **CROs** increasingly build in-house capabilities.
- Named targets include: **Ipsen, Amgen, Organon, Dr. Reddy's**.
- US-HQ companies are the priority (50%+ of global biotech R&D, 6 of 10 FDA approvals).

---

## 4. Customer Voice & Vendor Switching Triggers

### Incumbent Pain Points

| Vendor | Key Dissatisfaction |
|--------|-------------------|
| **IQVIA / ZS Associates** | Slow analytics iteration, rigid delivery model, high cost vs. perceived value |
| **McKesson / AmerisourceBergen** | Lower prioritization for smaller clients, shared-resource delays |
| **TELUS** | Launch-phase ramp-up challenges |
| **ProPharma** | Variable quality of contract MSL deployment |

### Key Switching Triggers

- Slow implementation speed and prolonged review cycles
- Lack of executive-ready dashboards and reporting tools
- Operational instability — inability to support lean teams at scale
- Therapy initiation delays and patient drop-off risk
- Reimbursement expertise gaps (prior auth, denial management)
- Insufficient scientific credibility in execution

---

## 5. Sagility's Differentiation

1. **Execution specialist positioning** (vs. strategy-heavy incumbents) — faster ramp-up, lower cost-to-serve, tighter SLA adherence
2. **Modular, pod-based model** for rapid capacity scaling during launch peaks without full-program overhead
3. **AI/automation embedded** in workflows (GenAI content creation, NLP-assisted coding, predictive analytics)
4. **Structured QA and compliance discipline** reducing audit and compliance risk in high-volume environments

### Illustrative Value Propositions (Ipsen Case Study)

| Service Line | Value Proposition |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **Medical Affairs** | AI-enabled medical content production & MLR workflow acceleration engine — reducing review cycles by 20-30% while maintaining compliance rigor |
| **Commercial S&M** | Digitally enabled commercial activation platform embedding compliant virtual engagement, AI-driven next-best-action models, and real-time performance steering |
| **Patient Engagement** | Integrated engagement intelligence and orchestration hub with predictive risk-based case prioritization to reduce drop-off, denial friction, and time-to-therapy |

## 7. GTM Strategy

### A. Marketing Engine

- **Conferences**: Anchor on 4-5 flagship pharma events:
  - Pharma USA (1.5K+ attendees, top-50 pharma)
  - Reuters Pharma (3K+ attendees, large pharma + vendors)
  - Veeva Commercial Summit (1.7K+ attendees, digital/CRM focus)
  - Americas Annual Meetings — Medical Affairs
  - World Drug Safety Congress — Pharmacovigilance
- **Media & Thought Leadership**: Feature stories, POVs, and analyst-aligned narratives in PM360, Fierce Pharma, MM+M
- **Analyst Relations**: Briefings with Gartner, Everest Group
- **Account-Based Marketing**: Targeted ABM for priority biotech/mid-pharma buyers

### Peer Analogs (Conference & Marketing Activity)

| Competitor | Approach |
|-----------|----------|
| **IQVIA** | Active analyst briefings, frequent pharma forum speaker, proprietary IQVIA Institute reports |
| **Syneos Health** | Strong analyst visibility (Everest PEAK Matrix), sponsors major conferences |
| **ZS Associates** | Frequent speaker at commercialization events, ranked in Everest PEAK Matrix |
| **Indegene** | Active sponsor, hosts proprietary Indegene Digital Summit, strong LinkedIn presence; participates in 30+ pharma conferences annually |

### B. Sales Team & Motion

| Role | Profile | Experience |
|------|---------|------------|
| **Commercial Lead** | Own pharma entry strategy, build SVP/VP-level relationships, govern pipeline | 15-20 yrs in US pharma commercialization; VP/Director archetype from competitors or mid-size pharma |
| **Domain SME** | Shape offerings, lead solutioning, design compliant frameworks | 10-15 yrs in pharma commercial ops; PharmD/PhD preferred; Director of Sales Ops archetype |
| **Hunter** | Drive new logo acquisition, qualify opportunities, advance deals to closure | 8-12 yrs in pharma services sales; BD Manager/Account Executive archetype |
| **Senior Advisor** | Accelerate C-suite access, refine sales narratives | 25-30 yrs in pharma; former CXO (COO, Chief Commercial Officer) |

**Sequencing**: Commercial Lead + Domain SME first (short-term) → Hunters as pipeline scales (medium-term) → Delivery leadership once anchor accounts secured (longer-term)

## Bottom Line

Sagility has a credible path to capture share in the pharma commercialization services market by:

1. **Leading with Commercial S&M and Patient Engagement** as execution wedges (strongest capability fit + highest customer openness)
2. **Targeting biotech and mid-pharma** companies dissatisfied with incumbent cost, speed, and flexibility
3. **Differentiating through AI-enabled, cost-efficient, modular delivery** — a deliberate contrast to the strategy-heavy, large-pharma-focused incumbents
4. **Sequencing investments** — GTM team and marketing engine first, then delivery talent and technology as pipeline materializes
